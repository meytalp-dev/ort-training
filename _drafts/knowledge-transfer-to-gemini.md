## The 5 Work Principles (5 עקרונות העבודה)

These are the school's foundational values:

### 1. האדם במרכז — The Person at the Center
Personal connection, sense of belonging, mutual respect. Every student has a meaningful adult who accompanies them. Activities: personal conversations, home visits, morning openings, weekly summaries, ceremonies.

### 2. רלוונטיות — Relevance
Connection to the student's world — emotion, environment, culture, dreams. Internal motivation through projects and world integration. Autonomy and choice in learning pathways.

### 3. תיקון הלב ותיקון עולם — Healing the Heart, Healing the World
Students as partners in building school culture. Values of compassion, justice, responsibility, moral leadership. Activities: weekly values ceremonies, citizenship program, community projects, Jerusalem repair project.

### 4. למידה דואלית — Dual Learning
Combining theoretical, emotional, and vocational learning. Real-world experience builds self-efficacy. Vocational tracks in hairdressing, automotive, and computers. Career preparation, army preparation, internships.

### 5. הובלה מקצועית — Professional Leadership
Staff as a living, reflective, initiative-taking professional community. Mentoring new teachers, multi-system team meetings, ongoing training, collaborative annual planning.

### The Graduate Profile (דמות הבוגר) — Pyramid:
1. **Belonging** — feels connected, seen, and respected
2. **Curiosity & Engagement** — curious, active, learns connected content
3. **Values & Responsibility** — ethical, contributes to community
4. **Professionalism & Excellence** — certified, experienced, ready for work
5. **Inspiration & Leadership** — motivated, aware, leads through partnership

# PART 7: SHARED CONTEXT & INSIGHTS

## On AI in Education
- AI tools should serve the teacher, not replace them
- The goal is to save teachers time on repetitive tasks so they can focus on relationships
- Students with learning disabilities benefit enormously from AI-personalized content
- Simple language, short sentences, lots of examples — always
- Every presentation follows the pedagogical structure (small knowledge doses, not text-heavy slides)

## On Teacher Resistance
- Teachers need to TRY tools themselves before being convinced
- Training should be hands-on: build something real in the first session
- The Claude Code workshop starts with a free 1:1 session (Session 0) for installation and first experience
- Show, don't tell — real products from real classrooms
- Meet teachers where they are — some are tech-savvy, most aren't

## On Content Creation
- Always research first, then create
- Fact-check everything (use Perplexity)
- Never publish about tools you haven't tried
- Content should be professional review, not sales pitch
- First person plural (we/us), warm and personal, never condescending
- Examples always include: teachers, managers, entrepreneurs, marketers — not just education
- Design inspiration: הדס (Hadas), Greg Isenberg, Ebony, Scoop (סקופ)

## On School Management
- Data should flow between systems (not siloed)
- Everything accessible from mobile (teachers are always on the move)
- Principal sees everything, counselors see their grades, homeroom teachers see their class
- "What you have" matters more than "what's missing" — especially for at-risk youth
- The student journey visualization moves FORWARD (scroll down = future), with mist for the unknown future (not locks)

## Key Dates
- School year 2026-2027 (תשפ"ז):
  - Term 1: Sep 1 - Dec 4, 2026
  - Term 2: Dec 15 - Mar 20, 2027
  - Term 3: Mar 30 - Jun 20, 2027
